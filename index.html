⚡ Quick tips

  • Search "CNG pump near me" on Google Maps — results show live status, timings and reviews.
  • Peak hours (8–11 AM and 5–9 PM) have the longest queues — plan fills in off-hours.
  • Most CNG stations operate roughly 6 AM to 10 PM; a few on highways run 24×7.
  • On highway trips, don't let the tank drop below a quarter — CNG coverage between cities is still patchy in some belts.

🗺 How to find a CNG station near you

Open Google Maps and search "CNG pump near me" or "CNG station". You'll get the nearest stations with distance, directions, opening hours and user-submitted info on queues and pressure. Many listings also show whether the station serves cars, autos or both — some have separate lanes.

CNG apps and official locators help too: IGL, MGL, GGL and Adani Gas all publish station lists for their cities on their websites.

🏙 Which Indian cities have CNG?

CNG is widely available in a growing list of cities and their surrounding regions, including:

  • Delhi NCR — one of the densest CNG networks (IGL and Adani)
  • Mumbai & Thane region (Mahanagar Gas)
  • Gujarat — Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot and more (Gujarat Gas)
  • Uttar Pradesh & Uttarakhand — Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Varanasi, Meerut and others
  • Maharashtra — Pune, Nashik, Nagpur belt
  • Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, West Bengal, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Assam — expanding networks in major cities and along key highway corridors.

Coverage changes fast — always confirm with Google Maps or the gas company's site before a long trip on CNG.

⛽ CNG pump timings & smart filling tips

  • Timings: most stations open around 6 AM and close around 10 PM. Highway and airport-area stations are more likely to run 24×7.
  • Beat the queue: early morning (before 8 AM) and mid-afternoon are usually the fastest. Avoid the start and end of the day when autos and taxis refuel.
  • Pressure matters: filling is best when station pressure is high — queues in peak hours often mean slower, partial fills.
  • Carry cash/UPI: most stations accept UPI and cards now, but cash is still the fastest at crowded pumps.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I find a CNG pump open near me right now?

Search 'CNG pump near me' in Google Maps — listings show opening hours. For late-night needs, look for highway stations, which are more likely to be 24×7.

What are CNG pump timings in India?

Most CNG stations operate from about 6 AM to 10 PM. Some highway and airport-area stations stay open 24 hours.

Which cities in India have CNG?

Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Gujarat cities, Lucknow, Kanpur, Pune and many more across UP, Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana, MP, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, West Bengal and other states.

Why are CNG queues so long in peak hours?

Auto-rickshaws and taxis refuel mostly at day start and end. Fill up mid-morning or mid-afternoon for the shortest waits.
